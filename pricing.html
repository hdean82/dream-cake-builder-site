Questions

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Every plan starts with 14 days free, and you don't need a card to start. Anyone can also try the Studio for 15 minutes without an account.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. There are no transaction fees. Add your own payment link, Venmo, Cash App or whatever you already use, and customers pay you directly.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Move up or down from your account page and the price changes from your next bill.

I use other order software. Can I bring my customers over?

Yes. Studio + Orders imports your customers, past invoices, supplies and recipes from a spreadsheet export of the software you use now. If your file looks different or something doesn't come across right, email support@dreamcakebuilder.com and we'll help you move it over.

Can I put the order form on my own website?

Yes. Copy the website code from the Order form page and paste it into your site. You can change the fonts, colors, answer boxes and corners so it matches your site. The Website Branding add-on ($7 a month) adds a cover photo, background photo, button and section styles, your own thank-you page and custom CSS, and removes the small Dream Cake Builder line under the form.

What is the Founding Beta Artist price?

Beta artists and the first 100 artists to join pay $19 a month for Studio + Orders, and that price stays the same for as long as you're subscribed.

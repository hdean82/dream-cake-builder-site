Questions before you start?

Who is Dream Cake Builder for?

Cake artists who want to develop designs visually and give clients a clearer way to review them.

What happens when a client wants changes?

Use the revision request to refine the editable cake, then prepare the updated concept for review. Once changes are requested, wait for a fresh concept before approving. Approval belongs to the specific revision shared, so an older approval does not automatically approve a changed design.

What happens after approval?

Open the prep checklist from the approved cake. Check off tasks and save progress there, then return to the approval record. Track the event date and mark the project Complete when the work is finished.

Can I explain non-edible decorations?

Yes. Decoration materials, serving status and client-facing notes help you explain what is edible, what needs confirmation and what must be removed before serving. The cake artist remains responsible for confirming these details.

Does this handle booking, contracts and payments?

Not yet. Today’s focus is cake design, client review, approval and preparation. Keep using your existing booking and payment process while those broader workflow goals are evaluated. New paid subscriptions are currently paused.

Questions

Who is it for?

Cake artists who want to show clients exactly what their cake will look like and get a clear yes before baking.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open the link you send, turn the cake in 3D and tap Approve or Request changes. You're told either way.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The Studio runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to download.

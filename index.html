How The Studio Works

From Idea To Approved Cake In Five Steps.

Every picture below is the real Studio, showing the same Blush Lambeth cake.

  1. 01

    Start Your Cake

    Pick one of 80+ ready-made themes, type the client's idea into AI Draft, or add their inspiration photo and tap it to pull the exact colors.

    ThemesAI DraftInspiration Photo
  2. 02

    Design Every Detail In 3D

    Set tier sizes and shapes, choose from 20+ frosting textures and 14 piping borders, then add Lambeth swags, bows, flowers and writing. Drag any piece where you want it.

    TiersFrostingPipingDecorLighting
  3. 03

    Send It For Review

    Tap Send For Review and text or email the link. Your client turns the cake in 3D on their phone, then taps Approve Design or Request Changes.

    ✓ Approved
  4. 04

    Share A Proof

    Make a one-page proof with three views, sizes, colors and notes. Print it, save it as a PDF or send it with your quote.

  5. 05

    Bake With A Plan

    The Summary shows servings, tier sizes and every detail tier by tier, plus a How To Cut This Cake guide. Save it to your Cake Box to reuse later.

Questions Before You Start?

Who Is Dream Cake Studio For?

Cake artists who want to show clients exactly what their cake will look like, and get a clear yes before baking.

Do My Clients Need An Account?

No. They open the link you send, turn the cake in 3D and tap Approve Design or Request Changes. You're notified either way.

What Happens When A Client Wants Changes?

Their note comes straight to you. Update the same design in the Studio and send the link again, so the approval always matches the latest version.

Does It Work On My Phone Or Tablet?

Yes. The Studio works in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with the cake on top and your tools right below it.

Can I Show Non-Edible Decorations?

Yes. You can mark decorations and add client notes about what is edible and what must be removed before serving. You remain responsible for confirming these details.

Is There More Coming?

Yes. We're building more tools for running your cake business alongside the Studio. Join the list to hear about them first.

Privacy

Your Information, In Plain Words

Last updated November 26, 2026. Questions? Email support@dreamcakebuilder.com.

What we collect

  • Launch list. Your email address, and whether you want news, when you join the waitlist.
  • Your account. Your email and sign-in details if you create a Dream Cake Builder account, plus the cake designs, orders, customers and settings you save in it.
  • Cake requests. If you send a cake request to an artist, the details you type (name, contact details, event date, your answers) and any inspiration photos you add.
  • Quote and design links. When an artist shares a quote or a 3D design with you, we record whether it was viewed, approved or declined, and any message you send back.

Why

Only to run the service: to let artists design cakes, send quotes and keep their orders; to pass your cake request to the artist you chose; and to email you about the launch if you asked us to. We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase stores accounts, designs, orders and photos.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Resend delivers our emails.

Each of them handles the information only to provide their service to us.

Cake artists and their customers

When you send a request or approve a quote, that information goes to the cake artist you're dealing with. They use it to make your cake, and their own business practices apply to what they do with it.

Your choices

  • Leave the launch list anytime with the link at the bottom of our emails.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing support@dreamcakebuilder.com.
  • Artists can delete orders, customers and designs from their account at any time.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Each artist can only see their own orders and customers. Photos sent with a cake request are stored privately for that artist. Photos an artist chooses to show on a quote, or in their public gallery, can be seen by anyone who has that link or visits that page.

Children

Dream Cake Builder is made for adults and cake businesses, and isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
